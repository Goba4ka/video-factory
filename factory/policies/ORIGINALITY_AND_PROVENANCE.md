# Originality and visual provenance

This policy is a blocking release gate, not a scoring bonus.

## Minimum original contribution

A video using third-party material must contribute a clearly new editorial
work: evidence-backed analysis, explanation, comparison, chronology, critique,
or a coherent storyline. The contribution must be visible in the approved
script and edit decision list.

The following changes by themselves are low-value and do not pass the gate:

- captions, borders, stickers, logos, or reaction faces;
- crop, mirror, speed, pitch, color, or aspect-ratio changes;
- stitching unrelated clips without a new supported argument;
- replacing a watermark or source label;
- synthetic narration that merely repeats the source clip.

## Provenance on screen

- Label archive footage, reenactments, simulations, stock footage, and
  AI-generated imagery when viewers could confuse them with the claimed event.
- Do not use a different person, place, date, conflict, treatment, or outcome as
  if it were the reported one.
- Preserve the source and license in the asset manifest even when no on-screen
  attribution is legally required.
- Generated media must never imitate documentary evidence of a real event.

## Release evidence

The final QC bundle records: asset ID, owner, source URL, license or permission,
source time range, on-screen label, transformation description, and the final
render checksum. A missing or ambiguous field produces `HOLD`, not a guessed
approval.
