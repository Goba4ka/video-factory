# Rights and source policy

This policy is fail-closed. A public URL is not proof of permission.

## Green lane

Assets may proceed automatically to editorial review when all are true:

- The item is the producer's original work, verified public-domain government
  media, or stock/Creative Commons media whose item-level terms allow the
  intended commercial edit on every destination.
- The landing page, creator, license, retrieval timestamp, and a local license
  receipt are stored.
- Modification and commercial use are permitted.
- Required attribution is ready for the destination caption or credits.
- No unresolved trademark, privacy, publicity, model-release, or
  property-release issue exists.
- The downloaded file hash is recorded and matches the timeline asset.

## Yellow lane: mandatory human rights review

- CC BY-SA or another reciprocal license.
- People, recognizable private property, brands, artwork, or performance rights.
- Platform-native Remix, Duet, or Stitch content.
- A permission message whose scope does not explicitly cover commercial use,
  editing, synchronization, monetization, platforms, territories, and likeness.
- News, documentary, archive, or institutional media with ambiguous item-level
  terms.
- A fair-use or quotation rationale.

## Red lane

- Downloaded TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, film, television, sport, podcast, or
  news footage without a suitable license.
- A clip with another platform's watermark.
- `NC` material in monetized production.
- `ND` material that must be cropped, captioned, recolored, retimed, or remixed.
- Music described only as "free" or "no copyright" without verifiable terms.
- An asset whose author, source item, or license cannot be reconstructed.

Red-lane assets never enter a render. They are replaced or the idea is blocked.

## Source precedence

1. Primary factual source and original media.
2. Official agency, institution, or subject press material with item-level terms.
3. Licensed stock.
4. Verified public domain or compatible Creative Commons.
5. Licensed UGC with a written release.

Secondary articles may help discovery but cannot be the only support for a
checkable claim when a primary source exists.

## Cross-platform music

A track licensed inside one platform is not presumed licensed on another.
The master render uses an owned or explicitly worldwide multi-platform track,
or no music. Platform-library music is added only to that platform's version.

## Content ID

Do not register stock, public-domain, Creative Commons, fair-use, or otherwise
non-exclusive media as a Content ID reference.
