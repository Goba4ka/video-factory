## Concept

One speaker is held in a near-black vertical frame while the camera closes in by degrees; acid-yellow words behave like warning signals, not decoration.

## Composition

- Focal element: the speaker's face and hands.
- Edge anchors: compact archive identifier at the top and a thin time rail at the bottom.
- Supporting detail: restrained phrase cards in the lower safe zone; the source's embedded captions remain visible as part of the supplied footage.
- Background: the real supplied video, globally monochrome with canonical grain and vignette controls.

## Typography

Oswald 700 carries the Russian caption phrases. IBM Plex Mono 700 carries only metadata. Both are bundled HyperFrames fonts and render offline.

## Do / Don't

- Do keep the real voice in front and the motion controlled.
- Do use hard cuts at the redacted source interval.
- Don't synthesize or clone the speaker's voice.
- Don't imply that upscaling restores missing source detail.
