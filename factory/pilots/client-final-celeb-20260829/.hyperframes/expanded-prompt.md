# Celebrity charity market — production expansion

## Style truth

- Canvas: 1080×1920, 30 fps, 25–35 seconds.
- Palette: `#0B0A0F` background, `#F7F2EA` foreground, `#BDB4B8` muted,
  `#FF4F79` single accent, `#A31D49` deep halo.
- Type: locally embedded Manrope for statements/captions; IBM Plex Mono for data.
- Mood: dark photographic editorial; energetic and contemporary, never tabloid-cheap.

## Rhythm

`HOOK-punch → proof-hit → concrete-hold → impact-build → human-payoff`.
Hard cuts follow narration punctuation. Every frame carries a slow photographic
camera move, while text lands quickly enough to be readable by the first half
of its spoken phrase.

## Global direction

- The face or photograph occupies 70–85% of the canvas. No floating web cards.
- Captions stay inside the central lower safe zone, at 70–86px, maximum two lines.
- Each caption chunk contains 3–5 words; the accent is limited to one semantic word.
- Depth in each scene: full-bleed photo, local coral halo, large ghost numeral or
  hairline geometry, then caption foreground.
- Motion recipes: `multi-phase-camera` for the photo world,
  `waterfall-entry` for phrase arrivals, `spring-pop-entrance` for evidence stats,
  `ambient-glow-bloom` for a bounded focal halo.
- Negative prompt: no top/side labels, no watermark, no fake social UI, no glass cards,
  no cyan-purple gradient, no tiny sources, no gossip claim, no black final frame.

## Scene 1 — hook

Concept: two recognizable faces occupy opposing halves of one full-height editorial
cover. The viewer understands “celebrity” before reading; the second line flips the
expected money story.

- BG: warm black + oversized blurred duplicate portraits.
- MG: Basta left, Egor right, each cropped face-first.
- FG: `ЛИЧНЫЕ ВЕЩИ` then `ДЕНЬГИ — НЕ ЗВЁЗДАМ`.
- Choreography: photo world slowly pushes; words cascade from below; `НЕ ЗВЁЗДАМ`
  lands in coral with a restrained glow.
- Transition: hard cut on the narration pivot.

## Scene 2 — scale

Concept: Ani Lorak holds the human focal point while `200+` and `2000+` appear as
physical editorial numerals, not dashboard widgets.

- BG: close portrait and a deep coral radial pool.
- MG: face on the upper 72%; evidence numerals lock across the lower middle.
- FG: short caption sequence.
- Choreography: camera settles; numerals scale in with `power3.out`; hairline rule draws.
- Transition: hard cut after `вещей`.

## Scene 3 — concrete lots

Concept: Burunov’s portrait feels like a premium magazine cover, then clothing texture
slides into the lower portion. Two real objects make the story tangible.

- BG: portrait / clothing-rack split with no UI frame.
- MG: the words `ЧАСЫ` and `ПЛАТЬЕ` as restrained display type.
- FG: captions remain dominant and readable.
- Choreography: portrait pushes in; object words waterfall; one subtle coral sweep.
- Transition: hard cut into the impact image.

## Scene 4 — impact

Concept: racks of clothing become a visual field for the verified number `4,6 МЛН ₽`.
The number is proof of prior help, not a boast.

- BG: clothes racks full-bleed with dark editorial veil.
- MG: `4,6 МЛН ₽` in IBM Plex Mono, occupying 65% of width.
- FG: social/environmental caption chunks.
- Choreography: camera drift; stat springs in without bounce; halo blooms behind it.
- Transition: hard cut on `Самый`.

## Scene 5 — payoff

Concept: three faces become one calm triptych, then recede behind the line that makes
the charity — not fame — the final subject.

- BG: Basta / Egor / Ani triptych.
- MG: a single coral rule binds the portraits.
- FG: `НЕ ИМЯ. А ПОМОЩЬ.` held long enough to read.
- Choreography: portraits settle; phrase cascades; halo holds; end on a living frame.
