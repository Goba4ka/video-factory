# Design — Black Staircase

Concept angle: every speaker removes one excuse until Arnold lands the physical metaphor: success cannot be climbed passively.

- Canvas: 1080×1920, 30 fps, 27.4 seconds.
- Focal element: tightly reframed monochrome speaker portrait occupying the upper 71%.
- Caption plinth: opaque black lower field from y=1360 to the frame edge; it guarantees clean Russian captions and hides any source lower-thirds.
- Edge anchors: thin oxblood line at the top; speaker name left; sequence number right.
- Typography: Arial Narrow Bold / Arial fallback, uppercase, compressed editorial tracking.
- Palette: bone white `#F2EFE8`, black `#000000`, oxblood red `#B5222A`.
- Background treatment: high contrast monochrome, subtle unsharp detail, grain and vignette.
- Motion: one continuous 3–4% push-in per speaker; two-frame white flash at each hard cut.
- Audio: original voices cleaned and leveled; procedural low drone side-chain ducked under speech.
