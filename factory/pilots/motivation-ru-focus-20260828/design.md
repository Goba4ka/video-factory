# Design — «Ловушка знания»

## Intent

Один уверенный русскоязычный спикер разрушает удобную иллюзию: ещё одна книга не заменит действие. Ролик должен ощущаться как личный разговор поздно вечером — сдержанный, тёмный и собранный, а не как шумный «успешный успех».

## Visual system

- Canvas: 1080×1920, 30 fps, 28.37 s.
- Palette: charcoal `#0B0B09`, warm ivory `#F2EFE8`, muted brass `#D9B978`, warm gray `#B8B2A7`.
- Type: Oswald 700 for statement captions; IBM Plex Mono for the editorial label and counter.
- Composition: face in the upper half, subtitles in the lower-middle safe zone, restrained top metadata. Two focal points only.
- Camera: continuous portrait crop from a real interview, slow push-ins with two short emphasis punches. No hard scene cuts.
- Grade: warm skin, lowered saturation, protected highlights, subtle edge vignette. No artificial VHS/glitch layer.

## Motion grammar

1. Continuous 1.00→1.05 push establishes intimacy.
2. A small punch at the phrase about being forbidden to learn creates tension.
3. The final phrase receives the closest crop and the brass keyword.
4. Captions rise 18 px and resolve from soft blur; no bounce or decorative motion.

## Audio direction

Original speaker audio remains authoritative and centered. Scott Buckley’s real CC BY 4.0 recording “The Long Dark” supplies intimate piano/strings and a measured late crescendo. The bed is carved under speech and kept emotionally present without masking consonants.
