# Design — Shadow Ledger

The frame behaves like an editorial contact sheet discovered in a dark archive. The speaker sits inside a clean horizontal evidence panel; the same image becomes a soft, nearly monochrome blurred field behind it. A thin muted-red rule anchors the composition. The lower third is intentionally quiet and dark so the Russian captions feel printed rather than stickered on.

## Tokens

- ink: `#08090B`
- paper: `#F1EEE9`
- accent: `#D5493F`
- muted: `#969394`
- headline: Oswald, uppercase, 72–84 px
- metadata: IBM Plex Mono, uppercase, 19–24 px
- caption line-height: 0.98–1.04
- safe margins: 62 px horizontal; 88 px top; 220 px bottom

## Motion

- Each scene uses a slow three-phase camera: neutral → restrained push → deeper push.
- New names/captions arrive as short bottom-up waterfalls with binary opacity and `power4.out` settling.
- Hard cuts at 10.241 s and 16.811 s receive a two-frame pale flash.
- The final action card holds for 1.60 s while the music opens; only a two-pixel bounded drift is allowed.

## Crop decisions

- Markaryan: face-on microphone footage throughout.
- Rybakov: clean face section from the same official video, approximately 148.0–154.57 s; the source subtitle strip is cropped entirely below the sharp panel.
- Hartmann: identifiable face reveal from 69–72 s, then his own action B-roll/face material from the quoted source.
